# Sexual Harassment at Workplace

## Category
POSH Law

## Relevant Law
POSH Act, 2013

## Relevant Section
Section 2(n)

## Last Updated
May 2026

## Meaning
Unwelcome sexual behavior at workplace affecting dignity or safety.

## Common Examples
* Sexual comments
* Unwanted touching
* Demanding favors

## Possible Punishment / Outcome
* Warning
* Suspension
* Termination

## Possible Remedies / Rights
* Internal complaint
* Compensation request

## Evidence Needed
* Messages
* Emails
* Witnesses

## Where To Report
* Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)

## How To Report
1. Submit written complaint
2. Attach evidence
3. Attend inquiry

## Important Notes
Complaint should usually be filed within 3 months.

## Source
https://www.indiacode.nic.in
