# Domestic Violence

## Category
Women Rights Law

## Nature of Case
Civil / Criminal

## Relevant Law
Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005

## Relevant Section
Sections related to protection orders and domestic abuse

## Meaning
Domestic violence includes physical, emotional, verbal, sexual, or economic abuse within a domestic relationship.

## Common Examples
* Physical assault by spouse
* Threats and intimidation
* Financial control and isolation

## Possible Punishment / Outcome
* Protection order
* Residence order
* Imprisonment
* Compensation

## Possible Remedies / Rights
* File domestic violence complaint
* Seek protection order
* Request shelter and maintenance

## Evidence Needed
* Medical reports
* Photos/videos
* Witness statements
* Chats/messages

## Where To Report
* Police station
* Protection Officer
* Magistrate court

## How To Report
1. Preserve evidence
2. Contact police or Protection Officer
3. File complaint before Magistrate

## Important Notes
Victims can seek immediate protection and residence rights.

## Source
https://www.indiacode.nic.in
