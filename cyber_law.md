# Identity Theft

## Category
Cyber Law

## Relevant Law
Information Technology Act, 2000
Indian Penal Code / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita

## Relevant Section
IT Act Section 66C

## Last Updated
May 2026

## Meaning
Identity theft happens when someone illegally uses another person's personal information such as Aadhaar number, PAN card, passwords, bank details, or digital identity for fraud or impersonation.

## Common Examples
- Using someone else's Aadhaar for SIM registration
- Creating fake accounts using another person's identity
- Stealing login credentials
- Using stolen debit or credit card information

## Possible Punishment / Outcome
- Imprisonment up to 3 years
- Fine up to ₹1 lakh
- Compensation claims
- Account suspension

## Possible Remedies / Rights
- File cyber complaint
- Freeze bank account transactions
- Request identity verification correction
- Seek compensation

## Evidence Needed
- Screenshots
- Bank statements
- Identity documents
- Transaction records
- Email logs

## Where To Report
- Cyber Crime Portal
- Local police station
- Bank fraud department

## How To Report
Submit complaint with identity proof, fraud details, and transaction records on cybercrime.gov.in or nearest cyber police station.

## Important Notes
Immediate reporting reduces chances of financial loss.

## Source
Information Technology Act, 2000

- Transaction history

## Where To Report

- Bank customer care
- Police station
