# Illegal Possession

## Category
Property Law

## Nature of Case
Civil / Criminal

## Relevant Law
Transfer of Property Act, 1882
Indian Penal Code / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita
Specific Relief Act, 1963

## Relevant Section
Section 5, Specific Relief Act Sections 5 & 6

## Meaning
Illegal possession occurs when someone occupies or controls property without legal ownership or permission.

## Common Examples
* Illegal occupation of vacant land
* Relative refusing to vacate inherited property
* Tenant refusing to leave after lease expiry

## Possible Punishment / Outcome
* Eviction order
* Recovery of possession
* Damages or compensation
* Criminal trespass charges

## Possible Remedies / Rights
* File civil suit
* Seek injunction
* Police complaint for trespass
* Possession recovery suit

## Evidence Needed
* Sale deed
* Property tax receipts
* Khata documents
* Photographs
* Witnesses

## Where To Report
* Civil court
* Local police station
* Revenue office

## How To Report
1. Gather ownership proof
2. Send legal notice
3. File possession suit
4. Request injunction if needed

## Important Notes
Do not attempt forceful eviction without court process.

## Source
https://legislative.gov.in
